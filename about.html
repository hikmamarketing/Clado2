About Clado Solutions

Practical IT education for a changing world.

Who we are

Learn technology from people who work with technology.

Clado Solutions is an IT training institute based in Kochi, Kerala, offering learning programs across Linux system administration, cloud platforms, DevOps and programming.

Our approach puts practical understanding at the centre of training. We aim to make complex topics approachable, encourage curiosity and help learners build the confidence to apply what they learn.
